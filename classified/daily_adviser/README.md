# classified/

This folder contains **copies** of important deliverables/data from elsewhere in the repo, arranged into a stable, easy-to-browse structure.

Nothing here is required for runtime. It’s meant for:
- quick sharing,
- human review,
- keeping “what matters” organized without breaking existing notebook paths.

## Contents

- `deliverables/best_of_2025/`: public-facing “Best of 2025” materials (also recommended to live in a separate public repo).
- `reports/`: audit reports (PDF/XLSX).
- `data/universe/`: investable universe CSVs and checkpoints.
