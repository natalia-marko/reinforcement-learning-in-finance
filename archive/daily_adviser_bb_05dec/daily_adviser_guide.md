Every morning (or once a month), update the CURRENT_WEIGHTS variable at the top and run the script.

Next steps:

1) Check the VIX (Volatility Index):

            Go to Yahoo Finance or TradingView and type ^VIX.

            Rule: Is VIX > 25 or 30?

            If Yes: The market is in panic. Trust the AI.

            If No (VIX is 13): The AI is hallucinating (bad data). Ignore the sell signal.

2) Check the QQQ Trend:

            Look at the QQQ chart for the last 5 days.

            Rule: Are there big red candles? Is the price below the 20-day Moving Average?

            If Yes: The "Fast Volatility" trigger (1.72%) is likely real. Trust the AI.

3) Check the News:

            Is there a major geopolitical event or a Fed announcement causing a crash?

            Context: The AI sees numbers, not news. If the numbers match the news, execute the trade.
